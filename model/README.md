# Body model

`body.bin` and `body.json` (format v3) are converted from **Universal Base Characters** by Quaternius,
Superhero Male, released under CC0 (public domain). The converter is `tools/convert_rigged.py`.

Each vertex is assigned to the muscle region of its dominant bone. Arms are split into biceps, triceps
and shoulders, and legs into quads and hamstrings. The figure is scaled to 1.76 m, with feet on the floor
and the front facing +z.
